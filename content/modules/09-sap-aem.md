SAP resells the Solace Platform as advanced event mesh, part of SAP Integration Suite.
This module covers how it's packaged and sold, with a light hands-on integration.

<Step id="packaging" title="Learn the packaging" minutes="60">

Review how AEM is packaged and licensed through SAP, and how it maps to the direct
Solace offering. Summarise the differences in your notes.

</Step>

<Step id="integration" title="Try a light integration" minutes="120">

Connect a flow in SAP Integration Suite to an AEM or Solace broker and exchange an event.

</Step>

<Step id="positioning" title="Practise the positioning" minutes="60">

Write and rehearse a five-minute positioning of AEM for an SAP-led customer. Record it.

</Step>
